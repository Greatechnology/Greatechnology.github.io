Key Calculator Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 15, 2026

Key Calculator is an Android app made by Greatech ("we", "us"). This policy explains what the app does with your information.

The short version

The current version of Key Calculator does not collect, sell, or share your personal information. It has no accounts and no analytics. Your data stays on your phone.

What stays on your device

  • Calculation history. The app saves your calculations on your phone so you can view and edit them later. We never receive them. Uninstalling the app or clearing its data deletes them.
  • App settings. Your preferences, such as vibration, are stored on your phone only.

Microphone and voice input

If you use voice input, the app asks for microphone permission. Your speech is processed on your phone by an offline voice engine. Audio is not recorded, stored, or sent to us or anyone else. You can turn off the permission at any time in your phone's settings, and the calculator still works without it.

Voice engine download

The offline voice engine is downloaded to your phone when you first open the app. Like any download, this request reveals your IP address to the server it comes from. We do not use it to identify you.

Other permissions

The app may use vibration for key feedback. This does not involve any personal information.

Advertising

The current version of Key Calculator does not show ads. Future updates may include ads. If we add ads, we will update this policy before release to explain what information the advertising provider may collect and how you can control it.

Children

Key Calculator does not collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will post the new version on this page and update the date above.
